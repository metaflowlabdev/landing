1. What AppFreeze does

AppFreeze is a menu-bar utility that pauses (freezes) and resumes background apps on your Mac to save battery and CPU. Freezing suspends a process in place; resuming restores it. AppFreeze never freezes the app you’re actively using, Finder, the Dock, or core system processes — by design.
