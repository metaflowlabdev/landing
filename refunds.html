Refund Policy

northox · Effective 12 July 2026 · Last updated 12 July 2026

The short version

  • Free apps have nothing to refund — you were never charged.
  • For paid apps, the refund is handled by whoever you paid: Apple (Mac App Store) or our reseller — Lemon Squeezy or Paddle (direct purchases).
  • Bought a paid app directly and it didn't work out? Email us within 14 days at northox.app@proton.me and we'll make it right.

This policy explains how refunds work for northox apps. Our catalog includes free apps, freemium apps (a free tier plus a paid upgrade), and paid apps. Where you buy an app determines who processes any refund. "We", "us", and "northox" refer to the maker of the apps.

1. Free apps

Apps and features offered free of charge — including the free editions of our freemium apps — involve no payment, so there is nothing to refund. You can stop using them at any time by uninstalling.

2. Paid apps & upgrades

When you pay for an app, an upgrade, or a license key, the payment — and therefore any refund — is handled by the seller you bought from:

Where you boughtWho handles the refundHow to request it
Mac App Store (Apple) Apple, under its own refund policy Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com
Directly from us (via our reseller / merchant of record — Lemon Squeezy or Paddle) The applicable reseller, under its refund policy, plus your statutory rights Email northox.app@proton.me or use the link on your receipt

3. Direct purchases — our promise

If you bought a paid app directly from us and it doesn't work as described, or simply isn't right for you, contact us within 14 days of purchase and we will issue a full refund of that purchase. To help us process it quickly, please include the email you used and, if you have it, your order number or license key. We process approved refunds through the reseller you paid (Lemon Squeezy or Paddle) back to your original payment method, usually within a few business days.

4. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under consumer-protection law — such as the EU/UK right of withdrawal for digital purchases, or comparable rights in your country. Those rights always apply on top of the refund options above.

5. What isn't refundable

  • Free apps and free tiers — there was no charge to refund.
  • Requests made well outside the window above, other than where the law gives you a longer period or the app is genuinely faulty.
  • Purchases made through the Mac App Store — those are Apple's to refund, not ours; we cannot issue a refund for a payment Apple collected.

6. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and post the new version on this page. Changes apply to purchases made after they take effect.

7. Contact

Questions about a refund? Email northox.app@proton.me.
